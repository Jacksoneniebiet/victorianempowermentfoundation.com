Victorian Empowerment Foundation (VEF) “SHE-EVOLUTION” is a women-led SDG initiative empowering young women through community, creativity, and capacity building. We equip women with the mindset, skills, and support to build sustainable businesses and purposeful lives — blending mental health awareness, self-discovery with practical growth to shape a new generation of confident, impact-driven leaders.

Our Ecosystem

“Three Platforms. One Vision.”

Victorian Empowerment Foundation (VEF): Advocacy and empowerment platform raising women leaders who lead with purpose and transform society.

Victorian Entrepreneurial Academy (VEA): A tech-driven academy where women acquire hands-on skills, mentorship, and incubate businesses.

Leadership Influence Roundtable Academy (LIRA): A roundtable where emerging and established leaders sharpen their influence, scale their platforms, and build systems that outlive them.

Lifelong Learning Principle

We promote a culture of continuous growth, encouraging students to seek knowledge in technology and leadership to adapt to evolving market demands.

Practical Application Principle

We emphasize hands-on experiences and real-world applications, bridging the gap between theory and practice in both technology and leadership.

Ethical Leadership Principle

We instill a sense of social responsibility, encouraging our students to lead with purpose and make decisions that benefit their communities and the environment.

Mentorship and Support Principle

We provide guidance and resources to nurture our students' entrepreneurial journeys, connecting them with experienced mentors and industry leaders in technology.

Global Perspective Principle

We prepare our students to navigate the complexities of a globalized economy, fostering an understanding of diverse markets and cultural dynamics through a technological lens. By adhering to these mission, vision, core values, and principles, the Victorian Entrepreneurial Academy aims to shape the future of entrepreneurship, enabling individuals to harness technology and leadership to make a meaningful impact in the world.

We Focus on Integrity, Innovation, Community, Excellence & Inclusivity

*Integrity: We uphold the highest standards of honesty and transparency in all our interactions, fostering a culture of trust and respect.

*Innovation: We encourage creative thinking and embrace technological advancements, inspiring our students to explore new ideas and approaches in their entrepreneurial journeys.

*Community: We believe in the power of collaboration and support, creating a vibrant network of entrepreneurs who uplift and inspire one another.

*Excellence: We are committed to delivering high-quality education and resources in technology and leadership, empowering our students to reach their fullest potential.

*Inclusivity: We celebrate diversity and strive to create an environment where all voices are heard and valued, ensuring that entrepreneurship and technology training are accessible to everyone.
